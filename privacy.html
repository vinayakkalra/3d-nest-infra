Privacy and analytics

Your information and your choices.

This page explains how the 3D Nest Infra website handles contact enquiries and optional website analytics.

Contact enquiries

Information you choose to share.

The enquiry form on this website does not save your information to a website database. When submitted, it creates a WhatsApp message containing the details you entered and opens WhatsApp for you to review and send.

Calls, emails, LinkedIn visits and WhatsApp conversations are handled by the relevant external service and are subject to that service’s own privacy terms.

Optional Google Analytics

If you allow analytics, the website loads Google Analytics to measure items such as pages viewed, approximate location, device and browser information, referring source and interactions with contact links. Analytics is used to understand website performance and improve the visitor experience.

Advertising storage and advertising personalisation remain disabled. The website does not intentionally send names, phone numbers, email addresses, company names or enquiry text to Google Analytics.

Your analytics choice

Google Analytics is not loaded unless you select “Allow analytics.” Your choice is stored in your browser so the website can remember it. You can clear your browser storage or use the button below to choose again.
