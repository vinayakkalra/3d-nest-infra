Privacy and analytics

Your information and your choices.

This page explains how 3D Nest Infra handles property enquiries through our website and Instagram, and your choices about contact and website analytics.

Contact enquiries

Information you choose to share.

The enquiry form on this website does not save your information to a website database. When submitted, it creates a WhatsApp message containing the details you entered and opens WhatsApp for you to review and send.

When you respond to one of our Instagram ads or message us on Instagram, we may receive your profile name or username and the information you choose to send. Our home search chat asks what kind of home you want, which areas you would consider and when you hope to buy.

Meta handles Instagram messages and may make them available to us in our business inbox. See Meta’s Privacy Policy for how Meta handles your information. Calls, emails, LinkedIn visits and WhatsApp conversations are also handled by the relevant external service under its own privacy terms.

How we use your enquiry

We use your Instagram replies to respond in the chat, help you shortlist homes and follow up about your request. If you ask us to follow up by call or WhatsApp, we use the contact details you choose to share for that follow-up. You can ask us to stop contacting you at any time.

Team access and sharing

The 3D Nest Infra team uses the enquiry to respond to your request. We do not sell Instagram enquiry information. This chat is for assistance from 3D Nest Infra, and we do not pass your Instagram answers to developers. If you separately request an introduction, we will explain what details are needed and ask for your permission before sharing them.

Keeping information limited and secure

Please avoid sending identity documents, bank details or other sensitive information in a home search enquiry. You can contact us to ask about the information you shared or request its correction or deletion.

Optional Google Analytics

If you allow analytics, the website loads Google Analytics to measure items such as pages viewed, approximate location, device and browser information, referring source and interactions with contact links. Analytics is used to understand website performance and improve the visitor experience.

Advertising storage and advertising personalisation remain disabled. The website does not intentionally send names, phone numbers, email addresses, company names or enquiry text to Google Analytics.

Your analytics choice

Google Analytics is not loaded unless you select “Allow analytics.” Your choice is stored in your browser so the website can remember it. You can clear your browser storage or use the button below to choose again.

Contact

To ask us to stop contacting you, request access to, correction or deletion of information you shared with 3D Nest Infra, or ask a question about this notice, email karan@3dnestinfra.com .
