Frequently asked questions

Property support in Mohali and Tricity, explained.

Clear answers for companies, executives and families beginning a local property search.

Which areas does 3D Nest Infra serve?

Our primary service area is Mohali and the wider Tricity: Chandigarh, Panchkula, Zirakpur and Dera Bassi, including Airport Road, Aerocity, IT City and the Sector 66-71 corridor. We also handle selected requirements in Gurugram and Delhi NCR through verified local collaboration.

Can you arrange corporate housing in Mohali?

Yes. We source executive residences, employee accommodation, furnished homes and corporate guest house options. Shortlists are shaped around security, commute, budget, tenure, furnishing and move-in timing.

Do you help companies find office or commercial property?

Yes. We support Grade A office, commercial building and operational-space searches, including requirement definition, micro-market screening, shortlisting, owner or developer coordination, visits and negotiation.

Do you support Global Capability Centre office requirements?

Yes. We support requirement-led office searches for Global Capability Centre (GCC) launch, expansion and consolidation requirements across Mohali and Tricity. The brief can include access, employee commute, infrastructure, security, floor-plate efficiency, fit-out readiness and future scale.

Can 3D Nest Infra source expansion or retail sites?

Yes. Our site-acquisition work includes local sourcing, owner outreach, initial property information and commercial coordination for retail, EV, institutional and network-expansion briefs.

How does a property search begin?

Tell us the intended use, budget, preferred areas, timing and non-negotiables. We filter realistic options before arranging visits, then stay involved through comparison, negotiation and documentation coordination.

Are the properties shown on this website current listings?

No. The images are representative brand imagery. 3D Nest Infra works from each client’s live requirement and verifies relevant options before presenting a shortlist.
