A practical buyer guide

Home-buying checklist for Mohali and Chandigarh.

A clear brief helps you compare the actual property, the full spending plan and the timing before arranging visits. Use this checklist for flats, villas, independent houses, resale homes and residential plots.

By Karan Kalra · 5 November 2026

Budget. Property. Location. Timing.

For buying requirements in Mohali, Chandigarh, Kharar and the wider Tricity. Start with your own priorities, then compare options against the same brief.

01 · Define the spending plan

Separate the property price from the rest of the budget.

Decide whether your budget is for the property alone or for the entire purchase and move-in plan. Ask for each quoted charge in writing rather than assuming the advertised figure covers everything.

  • Property price and exactly what it includes
  • Transaction charges, taxes and professional fees to confirm for the specific purchase
  • Parking, maintenance, deposits or society charges where applicable
  • Repairs, interiors, furnishing and the move itself
  • Payment timing and any financing arrangements to discuss with your lender

Agree on what you want to spend before comparing a lower headline price with a more complete quotation.

A useful first brief

Make the non-negotiables visible.

Share your property budget, preferred areas, use, bedrooms or plot size, move-in plan and three things you will not compromise on.

A family home, a home for parents and a future construction plot can call for very different shortlists.

02 · Compare the type of property

Keep the comparison specific to what you are buying.

Use the property comparison worksheet to record up to three options against the same questions, then save or print your notes.

Compare flats, villas and independent houses on a visit to see which checks differ by property type.

Ready-to-move apartment

Inspect the actual unit.

Record the layout and usable space, daylight, ventilation, privacy, parking, lifts, common areas and recurring maintenance.

Ask what is complete, what remains to be done, what documentation is available and when possession can actually be arranged.

Resale home

Compare condition and seller terms.

Record repairs, fixtures included, occupancy, seller expectations and the handover plan. Ask your advisers to review the ownership and transaction documents.

Put the price, any repair spending and the time needed before move-in on the same comparison sheet.

Residential plot

Start with the site and intended use.

Confirm the precise location, dimensions, access, surrounding development and available utilities. Clarify the authority and records relevant to that plot.

If you intend to build, assess the construction plan, permissions, cost and timing separately from buying the land.

03 · Use the same visit questions

Make each visit answer the same practical questions.

  • What area measurement is being quoted, and which document supports it?
  • Does the actual layout work for the people who will use it?
  • What is the real door-to-door commute at the time you would travel?
  • How do access, noise, privacy, parking and common areas feel on site?
  • Which repairs or works would be needed, and who handles them?
  • Which charges recur after the purchase?
  • What must happen before possession, and what is the proposed schedule?
  • Which questions and documents remain unresolved?

Photograph or record only with permission. Keep your notes about each property together so that a strong first impression does not replace a like-for-like comparison.

Local comparisons

How to plan home viewings in Mohali and Chandigarh.

Mohali: Group visits by the route you use most, such as Airport Road or the IT City corridor. For a flat in a large development, confirm the project phase, gate, tower and parking spot before the visit. Time the full trip from the front door, including the walk and lift.

Chandigarh: Group visits by sector. For an independent house, walk the approach from the street, check parking and stairs, and note any additions you want your adviser to compare with the property records. Keep the sector and property number with your notes.

Compare both cities against the same commute and move-in needs. Put up to three homes on the comparison worksheet and record any costs or checks that are still open.

04 · Check documents before commitments

Use official records and independent advice.

For a project enquiry, establish the exact project and phase, its current status and the documents being relied on. Check relevant records directly rather than treating a brochure, social video or an agent’s statement as the record itself.

The Punjab RERA public project search provides registered-project information and related records. For a property under GMADA, consult the official building-plan guidance and confirm which process applies.

Official lookups for Mohali and Chandigarh

  • Mohali. For GMADA areas, use the GMADA zoning and layout plans to find the published plan for the locality. Take the sector or scheme name to the site visit so you can compare it with the address you were given.
  • Chandigarh. For properties handled by the Chandigarh Estate Office, the Know Your Property lookup accepts a file number or a plot and sector number. Ask for those details before you use the portal.

Have your appointed lawyer, technical adviser and lender review the specific documents and questions relevant to the purchase. This checklist organises the search; it does not certify a property or replace that review.

Upcoming projects

Keep research tied to the current facts.

Ask for the exact project identity, current registration and launch position, available documents and the source and date of each claim.

Compare the available documents with the exact project or phase before adding it to your shortlist.

05 · Turn the checklist into a brief

Share enough detail for a useful shortlist.

Send your budget, preferred areas, property type, intended use, size needs, timing and non-negotiables. Add what you have already considered and what did not fit.
