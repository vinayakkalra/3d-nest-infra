A defined brief creates a useful pipeline

Local search discipline for multi-site and one-off expansion.

Expansion teams need relevant sites, clear owner interest and essential property information—not an unfiltered list of addresses. 3D Nest Infra translates the rollout criteria into a local search brief, conducts targeted sourcing and coordinates the early commercial conversation.

The client remains responsible for technical, legal and operational approval. Our role is to improve the quality of the opportunity reaching that assessment.

Site acquisition support can include

  • Target-area and corridor mapping
  • Property and owner identification
  • Initial owner-interest confirmation
  • Collection of essential site information
  • Visit and commercial coordination
  • Follow-through during client evaluation

Common questions

Site acquisition in Tricity.

What kinds of expansion sites can you source?

Defined requirements for retail, EV, institutional and other network-expansion uses.

What information is needed to begin?

Use, target geography, plot or built-up requirement, access, frontage, utilities, commercial model, rollout timing and non-negotiables.

Is a site verified before submission?

We collect initial property information and check owner interest before presenting a relevant opportunity. Technical and legal approval remains with the client and its appointed advisers.
