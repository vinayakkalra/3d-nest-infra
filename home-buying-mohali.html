How should you compare flats, villas and independent houses?

Start with the same commute, usable space, purchase costs and ongoing expenses for every home. Then check what differs by property type.

  • Flat or apartment. Ask which parking space comes with the home and what the monthly building charges cover. Try the lift and walk the route from parking to the front door.
  • Villa. Walk the outdoor areas you expect to use. Ask which facilities are shared and who pays to maintain them.
  • Independent house. Check the approach road and parking at the times you will use them. Note repairs you would take on and ask what utilities are already connected.

Common questions

Home buying, explained.

Can I contact you while my budget is still being decided?

Yes. Share the budget you are comfortable with now, your preferred areas and your plans. A range is fine if you are still exploring. We can refine the brief as your search develops.

What kinds of home-buying requirements do you support?

We help buyers compare flats and apartments, villas, independent houses, resale homes and residential plots. We also help buyers review available information about upcoming projects.

What should I share before starting a search?

Share your approximate budget, preferred areas, home or plot type, bedrooms where relevant, possession plans, intended use and non-negotiables. These details help us prepare a more relevant shortlist.

Which areas do you cover?

We cover Mohali, Kharar, Chandigarh, Panchkula, Zirakpur and Dera Bassi. The shortlist depends on your budget, location preference and timing.

Can I visit your office?

We currently operate as a service-area business without a customer-facing office. Contact the company to arrange a conversation or property visit.

How do I get current options and prices?

Send your buying brief to the 3D Nest Infra team. Current options, asking prices, availability and seller terms are checked for the specific property and date.

How are upcoming projects handled?

We discuss the available project information and its current status. Specific units, launch claims, prices and booking terms are shared only after the relevant facts and authority to market have been established.
