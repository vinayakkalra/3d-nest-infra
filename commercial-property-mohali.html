Global Capability Centres

Office search support for GCC launch and expansion.

A Global Capability Centre (GCC) is an enterprise unit that delivers specialised capabilities for a global parent organisation. Depending on the mandate, that can include technology, engineering, research and development, finance, customer operations, analytics or other business functions.

The workplace brief needs to support people, technology, governance and future growth—not only the first headcount plan. 3D Nest Infra converts those operational priorities into property criteria and a focused local search across Mohali and Tricity.

A GCC office brief can include

  • Launch, expansion, consolidation or build-to-suit requirements
  • Leadership access, employee commute and talent catchment
  • Power, data, security and business-continuity requirements
  • Floor-plate efficiency, fit-out readiness and phased growth
  • Parking, amenities, building management and operating hours
  • Handover timing, lease structure and commercial flexibility

Grade A commercial buildings

Compare the building claim with the operating reality.

“Grade A” is a commercial real estate market classification, not one universal statutory certification. We assess each shortlisted building against verified facts, the tenant’s technical brief and the complete lease proposition.

01

Location and access

Employee commute, airport and arterial access, public transport and visitor arrival.

02

Floor plate and fit-out

Usable efficiency, column grid, ceiling height, loading, handover condition and test fit.

03

Core infrastructure

Power availability and backup, HVAC, lifts, data pathways and building systems.

04

Safety and security

Fire-life safety documentation, access control, surveillance and emergency planning.

05

Operations and amenities

Building management, parking, maintenance standards, common areas and employee support.

06

Commercial structure

Rent, operating costs, deposit, lock-in, escalation, fit-out period and expansion rights.

Technical, legal, fire-safety, building-approval and financial due diligence remains with the client and its appointed specialists.
