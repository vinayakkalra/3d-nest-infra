Requirement qualification

Retail case note: qualifying a GIVA space brief.

A recorded retail brief for GIVA specified approximately 1,000 sq ft of carpet area and at least 15 ft of frontage. Those are separate checks: a headline area alone does not establish that a unit fits the format.

The next step is to confirm usable area and frontage, alongside the budget and floor preference, before presenting a property as a fit.

This example is at the requirement-qualification stage. It illustrates how we narrow a property brief before visits and commercial discussions.

The practical lesson

Define the measure that matters.

For a home buyer, useful measures may be bedrooms, usable space, parking, condition and possession timing. For a retailer, frontage and floor layout can be just as important as area.
